## 3. Disclaimed Myths & Folklore Quarantined

The following common commercial folklore claims were audited and **STRICTLY EXCLUDED** from the platform:
1. **"Living artisans cast the 1835 Cửu Đỉnh":** False. The historical founders lived in the 19th century; modern master artisans are their generational lineage descendants, not the original creators (enforced via Artisan Dignity Invariant).
2. **"Tự Đức tomb holds secret buried treasure in an underground lake":** Unverified folklore. Excluded from verified context and flagged in RAG guardrails.
3. **"Pháp Lam is identical to Beijing cloisonné":** Inaccurate. Huế artisans adapted European and Cantonese techniques to use pure copper and tropical-resistant silicate formulas.
