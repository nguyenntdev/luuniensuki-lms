## 3. Epistemic Uncertainty & Disclaimed Myths

1. **Unrecorded Foundry Names (1835):** Primary chronicles record the Ministry of Public Works supervisors and bronze weight allotments, but omit the individual names of Phường Đúc mold sculptors. The platform states this gap openly.
2. **Oral Folk Lineage (Thanh Tiên):** Paper flower crafting history is traced through village genealogy (*gia phả họ Thân*) rather than royal decrees.
3. **No Commercial Conflation:** Contemporary artisan workshops are strictly distinguished from imperial 19th-century court bureaus (Artisan Dignity Rule).
