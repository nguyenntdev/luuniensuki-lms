### 1.2 Core Financial Principles & Non-Negotiable Governance
1. **Separation of In-Kind Compute from Cash Operating Revenue:** Corporate in-kind sponsorship (BNIX VPS + Phổ Tuệ AI API credits) totaling **202,360,638 VND** is accounted for strictly as **non-cash infrastructure subsidy**. It is NEVER booked as gross merchandise value (GMV), customer prepayment, operating cash flow, or company valuation.
2. **Transparent Inquiry Funnel vs Simulated Checkout:** Until direct merchant payment processing gateways, banking reconciliation contracts, and cancellation escrow licenses are finalized, all user actions represent **qualified booking inquiries** rather than instant e-commerce transactions. Revenue is recognized only upon **verified completion of the experience**.
3. **Micro-Artisan Financial Protection:** Traditional artisans in Huế typically operate micro-workshops earning 6,000,000 to 15,000,000 VND per month. To maintain ethical alignment, the platform imposes **zero upfront listing fees, zero setup costs, and zero subscription charges** during initial pilots, adopting a pure success-based commission structure.

## 3. Funnel Conversion Architecture & Mathematical Formulas

### 3.1 The 5-Stage Customer Conversion Funnel

The commercial funnel tracks a prospective cultural traveler from initial digital discovery to completed, paid cultural immersion:

```
[ A: Unique Eligible Visitors ] 
        |
        v  (Engagement Rate: B)
[ B * A: Engaged Story Consumers ] 
        |
        v  (Inquiry Conversion Rate: C)
[ C * B * A: Qualified Booking Inquiries ] 
        |
        v  (Partner Acceptance Rate: D)
[ D * C * B * A: Accepted Booking Inquiries ] 
        |
        v  (Completion Rate net of cancellation: E)
[ E * D * C * B * A: Completed Experiences ]
```

### 3.2 Mathematical Formula Definitions

$$\text{Engaged Visitors} = A \times B$$

$$\text{Qualified Inquiries} = A \times B \times C$$

$$\text{Accepted Bookings} = A \times B \times C \times D$$

$$\text{Completed Experiences} = A \times B \times C \times D \times E$$

$$\text{Gross Booking Value (GBV)} = \text{Completed Experiences} \times F$$

$$\text{Platform Gross Revenue} = \text{GBV} \times G$$

$$\text{Gross Variable Costs} = V_{\text{AI}} + V_{\text{notif}} + V_{\text{ops}} + V_{\text{host}} + V_{\text{risk}}$$

$$\text{Contribution Margin} = \text{Platform Gross Revenue} - \text{Gross Variable Costs}$$

$$\text{CAC per Provider} = \text{Outreach Travel} + \text{Onboarding Material} + \text{Photography/Story Curation Time}$$

$$\text{Provider Payback Period (Months)} = \frac{\text{CAC per Provider}}{\text{Monthly Contribution Margin per Provider}}$$

### 3.3 Variable Parameter Descriptions & Benchmark Values

| Parameter | Symbol | Benchmark Value | Description & Empirical Basis |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Unique Eligible Visitors** | $A$ | 1,500 visitors/mo | Monthly web visitors reaching a specific heritage story or experience page via QR codes at monuments, organic search, or partner referral. |
| **Story Engagement Rate** | $B$ | 20.0% (0.20) | Visitors spending $>60$ seconds, interacting with the Cultural Bridge AI, exploring 3D models, or reading verified historical claims. |

## 4. Comparative Business Model Analysis & Pricing Experiment Design

### 4.1 Strategic Model Comparison Matrix

The platform evaluated two primary monetization models across six structural dimensions during field discovery with Huế workshop operators:

| Strategic Dimension | Model 1: Performance Commission (8% – 12%) | Model 2: Flat Monthly SaaS (1.5M – 3.0M VND/mo) | Winner & Strategic Assessment |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Incentive Alignment** | **100% Aligned:** Platform earns if and only if the artisan earns. Zero financial risk for the maker. | **Misaligned:** Platform extracts cash regardless of whether the artisan receives 0 or 50 bookings. | **Model 1:** Preserves trust with traditional craft families. |
| **Willingness to Pay (WTP)** | **Very High:** 100% of interviewed operators ($N=5$) accepted an 8–10% success fee on incremental guests. | **Near Zero:** 4 of 5 operators flatly rejected a $>1.5\text{M VND}$ monthly subscription upfront. | **Model 1:** Removes friction in provider onboarding. |
| **Seasonality Resilience** | **Naturally Adaptive:** In off-season (Oct–Jan monsoons), fee drops to zero; no burden on artisan cash flow. | **Destructive Churn:** During rain floods, operators pause operations and will cancel SaaS subscriptions. | **Model 1:** Ensures 0% seasonal churn during monsoon months. |
| **Cash Flow Predictability** | **Variable:** Revenues peak in Feb–May (festival season) and drop in Oct–Dec. | **Predictable:** Fixed monthly recurring revenue (MRR) stream for the platform. | **Model 2:** SaaS provides superior revenue predictability. |
| **Operational Complexity** | **Higher Audit Overhead:** Requires tracking completed workshops and post-session invoicing/settlement. | **Low Overhead:** Automated recurring credit card / bank transfer billing. | **Model 2:** SaaS has lower operational transaction monitoring. |
| **Expansion Headroom** | **Scales with Volume:** As artisan scales from 10 to 80 guests/mo, platform revenue scales from 516k to 4.1M+ VND. | **Capped Upside:** Platform revenue is capped at 1.5M–3M VND regardless of artisan success. | **Model 1:** Captures upside from high-performing experiences. |

### 4.2 Pricing Experiment Design: 10% Performance Commission Baseline vs Optional 1.8M VND/mo Flat SaaS

To prove rigorous commercial feasibility for RBPC 2026, LƯU NIÊN operationalizes a formal **Pricing Experiment Design** during the Huế pilot. Rather than guessing an arbitrary monetization structure, the experiment quantitatively benchmarks partner adoption, cash retention, and seasonal churn between two models:

```
+---------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------+
|                               PRICING EXPERIMENT ARCHITECTURE & COHORTS                           |
+---------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------+
|                                                                                                   |
|   [ DEFAULT CONTROL BASELINE ]                           [ OPTIONAL EXPERIMENTAL COMPARISON ]     |
|   10% Performance Commission                             Flat Monthly SaaS: 1,800,000 VND / month |
|   --------------------------                             ---------------------------------------- |
|   • Upfront Listing Fee: 0 VND                           • Upfront Monthly Subscription: 1.8M VND |
|   • Monthly Fixed Cost:  0 VND                           • Success Commission Rate: 0%            |
|   • Risk on Artisan:     0%                              • Risk on Artisan: High in low season    |
|   • Settlement: Post-experience verified completion      • Settlement: Upfront recurring 30 days  |
|   • Target: 100% of initial pilot onboarding             • Target: High-volume mature studios     |
|                                                                                                   |
+---------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------+
```

#### 4.2.1 Experiment Hypotheses & Success Criteria
1. **Hypothesis 1 (Zero Onboarding Friction):** Offering a 10% performance commission with **0 VND upfront commitment** achieves a $\ge 80\%$ partner MOU signing rate, whereas demanding a $>1.5\text{M VND/month}$ SaaS fee upfront reduces onboarding conversion to $<20\%$.
2. **Hypothesis 2 (Zero Seasonal Churn):** During Huế's severe monsoon months (October to December), 100% of partners on the 10% commission model remain active (0% churn), whereas fixed-SaaS operators would experience $>60\%$ voluntary subscription cancellation.
3. **Hypothesis 3 (Organic High-Volume Graduation):** Successful studios will organically seek a flat SaaS model once monthly commission fees exceed 1,800,000 VND/month, creating an artisan-initiated transition into predictable recurring SaaS revenue.

#### 4.2.2 Crossover Breakeven Mathematical Analysis

The mathematical transition threshold where an artisan benefits financially by migrating from the 10% performance commission to the **1,800,000 VND/month Flat SaaS Tier** is defined as:

$$\text{Breakeven Volume (Guests/Month)} = \frac{\text{Flat Monthly SaaS Fee}}{\text{Platform Commission per Guest}} = \frac{1,800,000\text{ VND}}{0.10 \times \text{Workshop Price}}$$

```
+---------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------+
|                     CROSSOVER VOLUME: 10% COMMISSION VS 1.8M VND/MONTH FLAT SAAS                  |
+---------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------+
| Partner Workshop                Ticket Price    10% Comm/Guest    SaaS Breakeven Crossover        |
+-------------------------------+---------------+-----------------+---------------------------------+
| Phường Đúc Bronze Foundry     | 650,000 VND   | 65,000 VND      | **27.7 (~28 guests / month)**   |
| Pháp Lam Royal Enameling      | 550,000 VND   | 55,000 VND      | **32.7 (~33 guests / month)**   |
| Thanh Tiên Paper Lotus Flower | 350,000 VND   | 35,000 VND      | **51.4 (~52 guests / month)**   |
+-------------------------------+---------------+-----------------+---------------------------------+
| Blended Portfolio Benchmark   | 516,667 VND   | 51,667 VND      | **34.8 (~35 guests / month)**   |
+---------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------+
```

*Financial Mechanics of the Incentive Curve:*
- **Below Crossover Volume:** The artisan is economically protected. For example, if Phường Đúc hosts 15 guests in a rainy month, their 10% commission is only 975,000 VND—saving them **825,000 VND** compared to a flat 1.8M SaaS fee.
- **Above Crossover Volume:** When a high-demand studio like Phường Đúc scales to 40 guests in festival season, 10% commission would equal 2,600,000 VND. By electing the optional 1,800,000 VND Flat SaaS Tier, the artisan **saves 800,000 VND/month**, while LƯU NIÊN locks in guaranteed non-seasonal recurring revenue (MRR).

### 4.6 Risk-Free Settlement Mechanics & Zero-Debt Working Capital Protection

To safeguard micro-artisans who have zero financial safety net, the pilot economics enforce three protective operational covenants:
1. **Zero Upfront Financial Demand:** Absolute prohibition of onboarding fees, listing fees, photography fees, or recurring software SaaS charges during the 90-day pilot.
2. **Post-Experience Bi-Weekly Reconciliation:** The 10% platform fee is settled strictly on a bi-weekly cadence (the 1st and 15th of each calendar month) after verified completion of the workshop. The artisan never advances cash to the platform.
3. **Cancellation & Deposit Protection:** If a traveler cancels $<24\text{ hours}$ before a session or fails to attend, the artisan retains 100% of any pre-collected material deposit, and LƯU NIÊN waives 100% of its commission fee.
