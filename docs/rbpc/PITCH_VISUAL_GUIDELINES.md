## 1. Visual Direction & Aesthetic Philosophy

The visual design for the RBPC 2026 presentation slides, product screenshots, and pitch video embodies:
**Contemporary Cultural Editorial × Trustworthy Service Design × Authentic Huế Warmth**.

- **Avoid:** Generic tech gradients, purple neon glows, heavy glassmorphism, pseudo-dragon ornaments, or fake 3D wireframe holograms.
- **Embrace:** Rich ivory paper backgrounds, deep charcoal typography, imperial pine green accents, terracotta highlights, and dignified documentary photography.

## 3. Presentation Typography Standards

- **Display & Section Titles:** `Playfair Display` (Bilingual Vietnamese/English support)
  - Slide Header: 36–44pt, line-height 1.25.
  - Subhead: 20–24pt italicized serif.
- **Body & Data Metrics:** `Inter` (Humanist Sans)
  - Slide Body: 14–16pt, line-height 1.5.
  - Callout Numbers: 48–64pt bold (e.g. `90%`, `202M VND`, `4.5 hrs/wk`).
  - Source Citations: 10–11pt mono or sans with clear brackets `[1]`.

---

## 4. 16:9 Slide Layout Grids & Composition

- **Aspect Ratio:** 16:9 Widescreen (1920 × 1080 px native resolution).
- **Safe Margins:** 96px on all sides (top, bottom, left, right).
- **Content Splitting:**
  - 50/50 Split: Left side = Editorial Problem / Sourced Claim; Right side = Authentic Product Screenshot / Living Artisan Portrait.
  - 3-Column Grid: For the 3-step value journey (*Discover* $\to$ *Meet* $\to$ *Experience*).
- **Visual Clutter Rule:** No more than 3 data points per slide. Every slide must feature a single primary takeaway heading.

---

## 5. Video Subtitle & Motion Timing Guidelines

- **Duration Limit:** Strict $\le 120$ seconds (targeted at 117.5s with a 2.5s safe buffer).
- **Spoken Pace:** 135 words per minute (clear, warm, articulately paced English narration).
- **Subtitles:**
  - Font: Inter Medium, 32px on 1080p video.
  - Style: White text on solid 80% opacity dark charcoal background pill box (`#242C2B/80`).
  - Placement: Centered horizontally, 72px above bottom screen edge.
- **Transitions:** 150–250ms smooth cross-fades; zero sudden flashy spinning transitions.
