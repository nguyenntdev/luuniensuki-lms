# EdTech Market Research (Web + Mobile)

## Snapshot
This scan focuses on mainstream learning apps with strong retention, personalization, and content workflows. It highlights recurring patterns and differentiators that map to Luuniensuki LMS priorities (activation, habit loops, content depth, and cultural immersion).

## Competitors and Pattern Highlights

### Duolingo
- Strengths: rapid activation, bite-sized sessions, streaks, leagues, daily quests, adaptive difficulty.
- UX Patterns: fast onboarding, immediate feedback, micro-rewards, session length under 5 minutes.
- Takeaways: prioritize "start learning in <60s" and strong streak mechanics.

### Khan Academy
- Strengths: structured learning paths, mastery-based progression, robust analytics for teachers.
- UX Patterns: skill trees, clear mastery states, practice loops.
- Takeaways: deep progress dashboards and mastery visuals improve trust and retention.

### Quizlet
- Strengths: spaced repetition, study modes (flashcards, learn, test), user-generated content.
- UX Patterns: recall-based practice, multiple modes from the same content.
- Takeaways: reuse content across modes and add spaced repetition clarity to users.

### Kahoot! / Blooket
- Strengths: social competition, live quizzes, classroom-friendly engagement.
- UX Patterns: immediate feedback, fast rounds, social proof.
- Takeaways: real-time or asynchronous competitions increase motivation.

### Brilliant
- Strengths: interactive problem-solving, high production quality, strong visuals.
- UX Patterns: guided discovery, visual explanations, step-by-step reasoning.
- Takeaways: add guided discovery steps and interactive explanations in lessons.

### Coursera / Udemy
- Strengths: high-quality content, credentialing, course completion incentives.
- UX Patterns: progress milestones, certificates, clear course structure.
- Takeaways: certificates and milestones create strong completion incentives.

### ClassDojo
- Strengths: classroom management, parent engagement, behavior reinforcement.
- UX Patterns: simple reward system, parent visibility.
- Takeaways: parent/teacher reporting can drive accountability and growth.

## Common Retention Patterns
- Streaks + daily goals as the primary habit loop.
- Progress visualization: skill trees, mastery bars, and weekly summaries.
- Personalized recommendations based on last activity and mastery gaps.
- Micro-rewards for small wins (daily missions, badges, XP).
- Social reinforcement: friend leaderboards, class leaderboards, shareable milestones.
- Lightweight onboarding with immediate first success.

## Product Gaps Worth Addressing
- Limited adaptive sequencing in heritage/culture learning apps.
- Few heritage apps provide mastery tracking or spaced repetition at scale.
- Low differentiation in content quality and media richness in this niche.

## Competitive Moats to Build
- High-fidelity 3D exploration and contextual stories as a unique learning mode.
- Strong content workflow (review, versioning, tagging) for trust and scale.
- Adaptive learning + recommendations tailored to user interests and progress.
- Mobile-first offline support for museum or classroom settings.
