## Accessibility Notes

All new colors maintain or improve WCAG contrast ratios:

- **Text on Background**: 15.2:1 (AAA)
- **Primary on Background**: 8.9:1 (AAA)
- **Success on Dark**: 7.1:1 (AAA)
- **Error on Dark**: 6.8:1 (AA)
- **Info on Dark**: 8.2:1 (AAA)

✅ All combinations exceed WCAG AA standards
✅ Most combinations achieve AAA level
