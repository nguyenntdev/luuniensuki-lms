## Key Improvements

1. **Warmer Gold** - The new gold (#E8B44C) is ~8% more luminous and warmer
2. **Deeper Backgrounds** - Create better depth and contrast
3. **Better Text Contrast** - Near-white text improves readability
4. **Harmonized Semantic** - All colors work together cohesively
5. **Modern Feel** - Overall palette feels more contemporary
