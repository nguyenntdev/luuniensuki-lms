## 3. Product definition and launch contract

### 3.1 Target customer and job-to-be-done

**Primary buyer (economic customer):** one small-to-medium cultural experience operator in Huế (craft workshop provider, curated tour operator, cultural host or hospitality partner). **Primary user:** cultural tourist on mobile with little knowledge of the subject. **Creator:** artisan or cultural storyteller who has **consented** to appear. **Initial pilot locale:** Huế; first vertical = one accessible, legally usable cultural experience (for example, an authorised craft-workshop provider).

**Buyer JTBD:** `When international tourists see our cultural activity but do not understand why it matters or trust our story, help us turn that interest into qualified booking inquiries without needing our staff to create multilingual heritage content from scratch.`

**Visitor JTBD:** `When I see an unfamiliar cultural object/tradition, help me understand the cultural meaning in my language, see the real people involved, and decide whether I want to visit or participate.`

**Maker JTBD:** `When my craft or story appears online, ensure I approve how it is told, know who shared it, and can see how enquiries reach me.`

All these are **hypotheses** until supported by field interviews; log negative evidence too.

### 3.2 Demonstrable P0 journey (≤90 seconds)

1. **Open** `/rbpc` on mobile without login: one clear proposition, genuine imagery/illustrations and CTA `Explore a cultural story`.
2. **Discover** `/rbpc/discover/[storySlug]`: a curated example with image, context, factual claim/source markers and `Listen` optional; heritage object not confused with contemporary product.
3. **Understand** `Cultural Bridge`: visitor chooses English or Vietnamese, asks a guided question or selects `Why does this matter?`; AI answers from an approved context corpus, attributes sources, expresses uncertainty and offers human review where appropriate.
4. **Meet** `/rbpc/people/[slug]`: authentic/permissioned creator profile with original voice and approval status, or clearly labeled sample persona if written approval is pending (sample must not be presented as verified real person).
5. **Experience** `/rbpc/experiences/[slug]`: transparent offer/provider details and CTA `Request a workshop` / `Ask for availability` (no claimed live inventory unless the provider actually maintains it).
6. **Request** visitor provides contact details **only with explicit consent**, gets `Request received — provider confirmation pending`, reference ID, privacy/retention notice; partner receives a restricted dashboard notification.
7. **Impact** `/rbpc/impact` or short partner view: count real opt-in requests and cite actual/estimated nature; show a **demo economics calculator** with assumptions clearly labeled. Never turn unactioned inquiry into revenue.

**Essential exit behaviour:** if AI fails, show approved human-written summary + provider inquiry CTA; if assets missing, show authored card with actual rights; if provider cannot be contacted, disable form and show `Not accepting requests yet` (not deceptive booking).

### 3.3 Scope by severity

- **P0 / must ship:** single story, two languages on published authored content, grounded AI answer or graceful static fallback, approved real maker/labelled sample, one offering, inquiry form, partner request inbox, mobile responsive, working build/tests, PDF/video, real interview notes, consent/IP gates.
- **P1 / desirable only after P0 green:** a second story, audio narration with approved voice, accessibility preferences, source-compare lens, simple partner statistics, QR deep link, anonymized engagement funnel; optional no-login web analytics.
- **P2 / explicitly deferred:** full ecommerce, fiat payout integration, token/NFT/smart contracts, multisite/tenant administration, generic marketplace search, complete LMS, AR reconstruction, 3D rebuild of all heritage objects, creator micro-royalties by LLM token, multilingual translation into unsupported languages, AI on-device voice cloning, auto-published content, geospatial live navigation, ads and social feeds.

### 3.4 Business success hypothesis

`If an approved story + Cultural Bridge AI + maker connection raises **qualified booking intent** relative to an ordinary informational page, and a provider accepts the fee/commission economics, the integrated experience merits a paid pilot.` **Do not pre-select success.** Define metrics and interview the operator before optimizing the funnel.

## 4. UI/UX MASTER SPEC — intentionally more polished than the source site

### 4.1 Design principle and specific failure modes to fix

The existing website shows a research-focused inventory (Cửu Đỉnh, Atlas 162, stories, maps, sources, historical networks, VR, quizzes, exams and accounts). That is useful for scholarship but cognitively expensive for a tourism business demonstration. **RBPC must be one editorially curated, discoverable narrative**, not an index of features.

**Experience principles:**

1. **Human first:** show real creator imagery, name and consent status before model diagrams, blockchain badges or a 3D hero.
2. **Meaning before mechanics:** headline states problem solved for visitors; engineering is progressive disclosure, not hero copy.
3. **One clear next action:** each screen has one primary CTA; all other actions quieter.
4. **Credibility in every claim:** documentary sources and creator attribution are legible without taking over the interface.
5. **Trust-preserving selling:** `Request` means request, not confirmed order or ticket.
6. **Accessible by default:** keyboard navigation, visible focus, readable type, translated essential errors, captioned media and reduced-motion settings.
7. **Cultural dignity:** avoid exoticising artisans, simulated ethnic dress, invented quotes, fake UNESCO affiliation or stylized reconstructions presented as photographs.
8. **Separation:** RBPC/demo shell remains separate from STTT and legacy learning paths; modernize and unify the **root LƯU NIÊN** shell rather than inheriting ten-item navigation.

### 4.4 Screen-by-screen interaction and editorial UX

#### Screen A — `/rbpc` landing

**Above the fold at 390px width:** nav, compact real image/illustration, eyebrow `LIVING HERITAGE, HUMAN STORIES`, single strong headline (`Culture is more than what we see.`), one-sentence value, primary `Explore a story`, secondary `How it works`. No autoplay sound, no registration wall, no infinite scrolling required to get to demo.

Below: `01 Explore / 02 Connect / 03 Experience`, creator preview, 1 sample approved story, short `Why trust this story?` (source + human review), partner CTA in footer. **Do not show metrics unless they are real and dated.** An elegant `See how it works` diagram is more credible than fake five-star testimonials.

#### Screen B — `/rbpc/discover/[slug]` cultural discovery

Layout: top 1:1 or 16:10 image with rights attribution, title and context, 1-minute approved summary, cultural connection card, one primary AI panel (`Ask about this story`), human reviewer/source drawer, related creator CTA. Separate `Historical context` from `Modern practice today`. Citation card should display source title, type, year, what it supports and link when legitimate. `No verified source found` is better than fabricated citations.

Two-mode AI: `Quick explanation` (≤120 English words) and `Explore deeper` (up to ~250), not 15 controls. Guided prompts: `Why does this matter today?`, `How is this made?`, `What's the difference between the historic object and modern craft?`. Avoid claiming that historic imperial objects directly represent the product currently sold.

#### Screen C — `/rbpc/people/[slug]`

Documentary-style portrait + name/role, short approved biography, recording/transcript optional, `Story approved by [creator] on [date]` only with valid approval; `Community verification` only with named authorized attester and evidence. Privacy-sensitive details omitted by default. Modules: `Their work`, `How the story was recorded`, `Meet them through an experience`, `Content use and attribution`. Clear acknowledgement that AI did not fabricate personal testimony.

#### Screen D — `/rbpc/experiences/[slug]`

Make provider identity, description, location at non-sensitive public granularity, duration, pricing if approved, booking/request availability status and accessibility notes obvious before CTA. If no live slot integration, the exact CTA is `Request availability`, **never** `Book now` with false immediate confirmation. `What happens next` explains partner follow-up timing; don't promise SLA without a partner agreement.

#### Screen E — inquiry form

Fields minimal: name, email or phone (one contact channel suffices), desired date (optional), party size (optional), message, explicit consent checkbox unchecked by default. Provide inline validation, server-side rate limit, spam mitigation that does not exclude accessibility users, and safe free-text limits. Acknowledge success with reference number, provider follow-up disclaimer, and a `Back to story` link. Never expose contact details via public predictable IDs; partner/admin access restricted.

#### Screen F — partner inbox

Authorized view only, list of inquiries with `new/contacted/confirmed/declined/closed` statuses, filters, creation times, export restrictions, legitimate access checks, deletion/retention workflow and audit trail. `Confirmed` cannot be automatically inferred from visitor form submission. **No revenue chart without real transaction source.** Analytics default aggregates only and does not show visitor PII to unapproved viewers.

#### Screen G — impact/evidence

Three explicitly separated sections: `Observed in pilot`, `User-reported feedback`, `Illustrative model`. If real metrics are unavailable, say `Pilot measurement planned` rather than 0, 100%, or fabricated uplift. Provide a source ledger and methodology link in the appendix, not a fake public report.

### 5.5 AI Cultural Bridge orchestration and source hygiene

1. Visitor picks **one published story** and locale. Never run free-text unscoped internet history generation in this demo.
2. Server validates the story is published, rights for AI retrieval are active and the visitor's question is in scope.
3. Retrieve only **approved claims/evidence excerpts** bound to this story and appropriate locale. Separate historic source, contemporary creator testimony, interpretive analogy and unverified claim.
4. Build prompt with explicit `source context is untrusted data`, token/length budget, approved `SOURCE_ID` allowlist and prohibition on fabricated craft/artist facts. Culturally respectful analogies must carry caveats.
5. Call existing provider/model abstraction with timeouts, modest retries, budget guardrails. Track aggregate usage and error types; **never expose AI key/credits**.
6. Validate cited IDs appear in retrieved set and each citation binds to a supporting claim. Reuse tests from `history_tutor` patterns but do not copy Cửu Đỉnh-specific prompt verbatim. Parse unsupported/deceptive facts and fail closed to prepared summary.
7. Render answer with readable inline citations, source drawer, reviewer/corpus context and `AI-assisted, based on approved material`.
8. Respect consent for recorded voices/stories; no speaker cloning without separate explicit permission. Any multilingual translation must be approved for publishing where culturally sensitive.
9. Cache only approved, rights-valid excerpts. Invalidate when a rights holder revokes the licence or an editor publishes a correction; keep version identifier in cache key.
10. For unavailable models, graceful static `approved_summary_en` / `approved_summary_vi` and a clear message.

**AI eval set before any public claims:** at least 24–36 small, **human-authored** prompts covering 6 basic factual, 6 contextual/cultural, 6 cross-cultural comparisons, 4 maker-rights/identity, 4 adversarial/prompt injection and 4 unsupported/out-of-scope cases (adjust exact count after human review). Reviewers mark `grounded`, `citation-valid`, `non-fabricating`, `respectful`, `clear`, `appropriate uncertainty`, language quality. Record denominators, model/version/cost and known failures; do not claim industry-grade benchmark from this sample.

**Target release bar (internal, not external metric):** 0 known fabricated citations in reviewed sample, 0 private leaks, 0 false booking confirmation; ≥90% eligible test questions with reviewed acceptable answers **or** safe fallback; all adversarial cases block cross-scope data; model timeout does not block visitor flow. If not met, disable interactive AI on demo and show controlled examples/approved static responses with truthful labels.

### 5.6 Security/privacy/RBAC and anti-abuse

- Public APIs allowlist published data and evaluate rights **on every request**, not only during publication. Drafts, partner contacts, interview notes never appear in public JSON or Next.js server-rendered HTML.
- Server-side RBAC and provider ownership; fuzz IDs; test horizontal privilege escalation. Do not rely on hidden buttons as access controls.
- Limit input field size, sanitise rich text, safe external URLs, prevent XSS, SSRF and prompt injection; escape markdown/model output. Do not embed arbitrary images, redirects, or links suggested by LLM.
- Inquiry route has IP/user/session rate limit, abuse thresholds, CAPTCHA alternative that permits legitimate access, replay/idempotency protection, bounded retention and log redaction.
- Strong privacy notice and separate explicit consent for sending details to partner versus analytics/marketing. Default no marketing opt-in. Children's data: no unnecessary birthdates, classroom records or sensitive profiles in public pilot.
- Financial simulations use sample numbers and stay sandboxed; no real payment storage or checkout.
- New endpoints have OpenAPI contract and automated tests; backend route must be visible through actual Rust gateway and Nginx.
- Feature flag `RBPC_ENABLED`, `RBPC_AI_ENABLED`, `RBPC_INQUIRY_ENABLED` default to safe values. `RBPC_MOCK_DATA` must never silently be active in public production/demo environment; any demo-only source visibly watermarked.
- Backup/rollback plan; production secrets remain GitHub environment/secrets manager, never logs, never markdown artifacts.

## 6. Market research & customer validation — do not automate human evidence

### 6.1 Discovery hypotheses to falsify

- `H1 — Unmet comprehension`: a meaningful share of international visitors cannot explain why the selected cultural experience matters after reading a normal provider description.
- `H2 — Human connection`: showing a real creator and her/his own approved story produces more meaningful interest than generic heritage content.
- `H3 — Business problem`: a provider spends significant resources writing/translating content, responding to low-quality enquiries or convincing visitors to book.
- `H4 — Willingness to pay`: at least one provider can articulate a plausible fee or commission they would accept **conditional on incremental qualified demand**.
- `H5 — Rights and dignity`: creators approve the format and have the capacity to refuse/revise an AI-assisted portrayal.
- `H6 — Cost feasibility`: after sponsored credits end, the AI/hosting/care unit costs can be covered by defensible partner economics.
- `H7 — Counterfactual`: any booking interest is truly incremental, not simply diverted from existing FB/Zalo/OTA purchases.

### 6.2 Target discovery sample — planning targets, not achieved counts

**Due to short sprint:** aim for **4–6** cultural experience providers (owner/manager/booking operator), **2–3** creators/artisans (original first-party story authors), and **12–20** visitor/prospective visitor interviews or usability trials. Record **actual achieved n**; smaller, honest samples are better than fabricated or forced quotas. Use a 10–15 min interview and follow-up comprehension task. Ask a parent/guardian or supervising institution about appropriate consent if researching minors. Keep raw identifying recordings outside repository.

**Recruitment:** local visitor operators, cultural workshop providers, hotels/guesthouses, craft studios, heritage researchers with rights to speak; local-language and English audiences. The same interview should not count as independent buyer + user evidence unless those roles genuinely overlap. Keep sponsor organisations out of the 'customer willing-to-pay' category unless they are explicitly considering purchases.

**Provider questions (do not lead):** `How do visitors find your workshop today?` `What makes an enquiry qualified?` `How many inquiries actually book?` `Which languages require manual work?` `What does that cost in time/money?` `When did a customer misunderstand an experience?` `What channels have you tried?` `Would you share your pricing?` `What would make a 10% commission unacceptable?` `Do you prefer a monthly service or pay-per-booking?` `What data can't you share?` `Would you pilot if no sales are guaranteed?`.

### 6.3 Usability experiment design (minimum pilot)

Define an **ordinary content baseline**: a static page containing the same provider facts and price. Define **treatment**: approved Cultural Bridge interpretation and creator connection. Metrics: task completion; time to first meaningful explanation; comprehension scored by independent mini-rubric; inquiry-click intent; actual provider-accepted inquiry if available; visitor confidence; explanatory quality. Record confounders, sample dates, language and interview script version.

**Data deliverables:** `docs/rbpc/VALIDATION.md` with evidence IDs `EVID-UX-001`, `EVID-PARTNER-001`, date, sample count, verbatim anonymized quotes, negative findings, research limitations and proposed product changes. **No product–market-fit claims** with 1–2 supportive comments. Partner email showing interest is `pilot interest`, not `paid pilot`. LOI is nonbinding unless text clearly says otherwise. Do not fabricate future MOU.

### 7.3 Unit economics worksheet (spreadsheet / business-plan appendix design)

Columns and formulas — *all inputs have sources and dated assumptions*:

```
A = Unique eligible visitors per provider per month
B = Story engagement rate
C = Qualified inquiry rate among engaged visitors
D = Partner acceptance / booking confirmation rate
E = Completion rate after confirmation (net of cancellation/refund)
F = Average eligible completed booking value (VND)
G = Agreed platform fee rate (commission basis)

Qualified inquiries       = A * B * C
Completed bookings        = A * B * C * D * E
Gross booking value       = Completed bookings * F
Platform gross revenue    = Gross booking value * G
Gross variable costs      = model tokens + payment processing (if platform collects)
                            + partner servicing + hosting variable + refunds/risk reserve
Contribution margin      = Platform gross revenue - Gross variable costs
Acquisition cost/provider = founder outreach + transport + sales time + onboarding material
Payback period           = Acquisition cost/provider / monthly provider contribution
Business break-even      = fixed overhead / total contribution per provider (if > 0)
```

**Sensitivity:** independent low/base/high cases for visitor traffic, inquiry conversion, completion rate, commission and AI cost. Include zero-booking scenario, provider churn and end of sponsorship. If no actual measured denominators, use `illustrative only — assumptions, not results`; **do not** generate hyper-precise three-year forecasts from wishful inputs.

**Numerical worked EXAMPLE, deliberately fictional:** 30 completed workshop bookings × 700,000 VND × 10% commission → 2,100,000 VND gross platform revenue **before** all costs. Only an example, not proof of 30 real bookings; if these 30 bookings would have happened anyway, incremental value may be near zero.

**Minimum data S3 must gather:** actual workshop advertised price, rough provider capacity, known inquiry channels, cancellation patterns, cost of producing bilingual story, inference cost per question, customer-support effort, contract/payment constraints. Do not infer partner economics solely from country-wide tourist arrival counts.

### V2-B.3 Curation pipeline: researcher first, AI assistant second

1. **Discover/Inventory** place candidates from official UNESCO map, provincial and Hue Monuments Conservation Centre sources; record source URLs and access date, entity type, designation and place/parent relationship. Imported scraped text is **not** proof of reuse rights.
2. **Normalize** naming/aliases (`Ngọ Môn` vs `Meridian Gate`), dates as ranges with source references, historical name/state, location precision, and separate distinct object vs containing site.
3. **Evidence extraction**: AI may propose `claim, proposed_source, cited_page, confidence, unresolved_question` in an unpublished workspace; editor must verify against actual source text. Never let fabricated page numbers, reconstructed facts or copied copyrighted text into public dataset.
4. **Research review**: one named content editor plus independent reviewer for featured pages; no invented expert signatures; audit approvals. Mark each field factual vs interpretation vs folklore vs disputed.
5. **Rights review**: images/audio/model licence exact by asset, content permission separate from source factual validity. Source can be read for facts without right to reproduce whole article/media.
6. **Translate** via source-aware cultural adaptation, then native/reviewer check; no AI translation presented as licensed/approved until review. Historical proper nouns locked.
7. **Publish** only approved stable version; index/cache refresh with source metadata; withdrawal/revocation cascades to AI search and pages.
8. **Evaluate** continuously: broken links, changed copyright terms, wrong-period imagery, geographic inconsistencies, stale access/opening status, AI citation attachment and opt-out requests.

For each asset, a student reviewer must record `license_at_review, model URL, creator, restrictions, embed terms, noai, date, attribution, intended purpose (website/competition video/commercial site), approval/evidence file`. Third-party thumbnails/screenshots in pitch video can have different permissions from live embed: obtain permission or use own lawful imagery.

**NoAI:** where Sketchfab page says `NoAI`, prohibit use of model/texture/renders as generative AI training data, development inputs, prompts, automated computer vision feature extraction or embedding corpus. Normal viewer embedding, if allowed, is **not** permission to feed the rendered scene to LLM. Avoid invisible background asset crawling/download APIs; do not strip Sketchfab branding or watermark. Never infer historical authenticity from model account name.

**Technical viewer policy:** store official direct model URL + UUID when extractable, not third-party iframe HTML; build safe iframe from allowlisted `https://sketchfab.com/models/{uuid}/embed` *only when rights reviewed and frame confirmed*, with `title` + `loading="lazy"` + restricted `allow` policy necessary for fullscreen/VR only, `referrerPolicy` consistent with provider terms; no auto-play on mobile, fixed aspect ratio, close/stop button, preconnect only upon intent, skeleton and timeout fallback. Host sandbox may block iframe; always `Open on Sketchfab` link and 2D image alternative. Add optional `model_type=photogrammetry|survey|reconstruction|illustrative`, `accuracy_state=unreviewed|reference_checked|historian_reviewed|illustrative_only`, restoration-era provenance, creator/credits, and link to source. If a model is of one courtyard or a stele building, do not label it full tomb/whole city.

**Safety/performance:** no mandatory Sketchfab iframe in homepage critical rendering path; lazy load below fold; clear iframe on close; CSP `frame-src https://sketchfab.com` only with product/security review; `connect-src` not opened broadly; user-initiated external embed and privacy disclosure if cookies/tracking; WebGL failure fallback. Check no camera autoplay, icon controls reachable via keyboard, frame title describes model. **Never reverse-engineer/extract GLB from Sketchfab embedding**. For rights-approved download, use documented download method and archive proof of rights and original author, not scrape.

### V2-C.3 Rights decision gate per media asset

**States:** `discovered → candidate → identity_verified → historical_reviewed → rights_reviewed → approved_embed/approved_local_file → published` OR `blocked_nc / blocked_noai / rejected_accuracy / broken_link / withdrawn`.

**Acceptance algorithm:**

```text
IF precise heritage object/site identity not sufficiently supported:
    do NOT associate 3D with site as documentary proof
IF original URL missing, UUID malformed, or page no longer resolvable:
    reject integration, show licensed still image
IF rights label is CC BY-NC(-SA), Editorial, NoAI-restricted for intended use,
    or creator rights ambiguous:
    do NOT use in commercial imagery or AI input; request explicit grant or fallback
IF embedding allowed under Sketchfab's current conditions and material rights:
    prefer creator-hosted lazy iframe, visible credit, source link and usage notice
IF local 3D file redistribution allowed separately with rights proof:
    consider self-host for performance after actual security/asset review
ELSE:
    published page has still-image/provenance pathway and external link when lawful
```

This is a conservative internal policy, not final legal advice. Even a CC BY model still requires actual terms and rights checks; the model creator may incorporate third-party photogrammetry. **Permission to show embedded is distinct from permission to train AI on content**.
