## Deduplication decision protocol
1. For every paper: freeze RQ, intended external evidence, estimand, novelty owner, principal table/figure and author overlap ledger BEFORE analysis.
2. Two papers may share code and input data, not the same primary hypothesis plus primary outcome table. Disclose reuse and cite predecessor.
3. If paper provides only a renamed benchmark/feature, merge, downgrade to supplementary material or withdraw. P04 and P08 are explicit conditional projects.
4. Run `V3_CLAIMS_LEDGER.csv` audit with two independent reviewers; primary contribution collisions are block gates.
5. A method result on synthetic heritage policy must never be reported as real student outcome, model generalization or expert judgment.
