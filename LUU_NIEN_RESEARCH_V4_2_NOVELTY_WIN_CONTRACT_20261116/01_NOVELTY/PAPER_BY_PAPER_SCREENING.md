# 14-paper novelty and win dossier — preliminary verdict

## Methodology
Overlaps below are **conceptual screening**, not final originality adjudication. A method is permitted only when old V3 errors are reproduced and the strongest baseline has a verified residual failure. Exact generic techniques known in prior art are explicitly rejected as novel contributions. Science success is not equivalent to beating a model: some papers are infrastructure/measurement/security studies.

## Strongest warnings
- **P01:** Generic conformal+group/cost deferral overlaps direct 2026 methods. Need genuinely distinct cohort-capacity risk constraints and mathematically proved new property; otherwise only an application.
- **P05:** Forgetting attention/length extrapolation and Hawkes temporal effects have direct prior art. Novelty only possible beyond LefoKT, HawkesKT, recency-aware approaches in actual question-level KT.
- **P06:** Fair budget allocation / robust optimization are not novel. Stronger estimand needs REAL allocation observations and fair tradeoff.
- **P07/P14:** Both concern media; P07 must optimize rights-valid retrieval, P14 assess semantic accessibility. Do not reuse same primary claim.
- **P09/P12:** Both are historical source verification. P09 is retrieval/sufficiency at claim answer-level; P12 is edition/page-specific archival traceability + chain-of-custody. Merge if same corpus, metrics and model dominate both.
- **P13:** Unless actual SRL instruments are available, rename claim to early warning prediction; propose only after click/GBDT/temporal baseline gap.
- **P02/P08/P10/P11/P14:** Evaluate exact proof, safety, human validity or measurement, not fake neural wins.
