# Closest-work evidence index — initial public-source review, 2026-11-16

These references were checked through public source pages and/or present in the V4.1 pack. Existence and broad mechanisms are verified; licence, code hash, rerun and definitive novelty exclusion **remain pending**. This is NOT an exhaustive systematic review.

- `CRC2026` — Kwon & Kim (2026), Conformal selective prediction with cost aware deferral for safe clinical triage under distribution shift. https://www.nature.com/articles/s41598-026-40637-w. **Relevance:** Direct conceptual overlap: risk calibration + conditional deferral under shift; not an education baseline.
- `LEFOKT` — Bai et al. (2025), Rethinking and Improving Student Learning and Forgetting Processes for Attention based KT. https://ojs.aaai.org/index.php/AAAI/article/view/34998. **Relevance:** Attention forgetting + length extrapolation already studied.
- `KT2026` — Beyond AUC: Evaluating Deep Knowledge Tracing Through Temporal Coherence and Post Hoc Explanations (2026). https://www.mdpi.com/2079-9292/15/21/4975. **Relevance:** Recent comparator/evaluation protocol; verify compatible split.
- `HKT` — Knowledge Tracing: A Survey (ACM). https://doi.org/10.1145/3569576. **Relevance:** HawkesKT and forgetting-aware models exist.
- `SIMPLEKT` — simpleKT: A Simple but Tough-to-beat Baseline for Knowledge Tracing. https://pykt.org/simplekt. **Relevance:** Strong established reference; benchmark setup must be aligned.
- `PYKT` — pyKT: standardized knowledge tracing toolkit. https://github.com/pykt-team/pykt-toolkit. **Relevance:** Reference runner and candidate baselines, pin hashes.
- `MATH_TUTOR` — Macina et al. (2025), MathTutorBench, EMNLP. https://aclanthology.org/2025.emnlp-main.11/. **Relevance:** Open-ended generated tutoring quality, beyond decision classification.
- `TUTOR_SURVEY_2026` — What makes an LLM a tutor? A component-centric survey of LLM-based and agentic tutoring systems (2026). https://link.springer.com/article/10.1007/s44336-026-00047-y. **Relevance:** Pedagogy benchmarks differ and rank concordance limited.
- `OR_TOOLS` — Google OR-Tools CP-SAT official documentation. https://developers.google.com/optimization/cp/cp_solver. **Relevance:** Exact solver reference; match objective and proof.
- `FAIR2024` — Verma et al. (2024), Group Fairness in Predict-Then-Optimize Settings for Restless Bandits, UAI. https://proceedings.mlr.press/v244/verma24a.html. **Relevance:** Learning and fair budget allocation already published; task assumptions differ.
- `AV2025` — AVerImaTeC (NeurIPS 2025), image-text claims with evidence. https://proceedings.neurips.cc/paper_files/paper/2025/hash/0695cbd1f85f54780e2b351db4252a97-Abstract-Datasets_and_Benchmarks_Track.html. **Relevance:** Temporal leakage, evidence sufficiency and human annotation precedent.
- `COVEGAT` — Bader et al. (2025), CoVeGAT citation-aligned claim verification. https://aclanthology.org/2025.r2lm-1.1/. **Relevance:** Claim-citation graph verification not by itself new.
- `AV2024` — AVeriTeC shared task (2024). https://aclanthology.org/2024.fever-1.1/. **Relevance:** Source retrieval, verification and QA-evidence methods.
- `FACTSCORE` — FActScore (EMNLP 2023). https://aclanthology.org/2023.emnlp-main.741/. **Relevance:** Atomic claim factual precision prior art.
- `SIGLIP2` — SigLIP 2 multilingual vision-language encoders (2025). https://arxiv.org/abs/2502.14786. **Relevance:** Competitive multimodal retrieval backbone.
- `SRL2025` — Boulahmel et al. (2025), Investigating Self-Regulated Learning Measurement Based on Trace Data. https://doi.org/10.1007/s10758-025-09816-y. **Relevance:** Construct validity cannot be inferred from clicks.
- `SRLREVIEW2025` — Nath et al. (2025), Balancing Contextualization and Generalization in Trace-based Measurement of SRL. https://doi.org/10.3102/00346543251382238. **Relevance:** Explicit validation and contextualization requirements.
- `SRLRUBRIC` — De Barba et al. (2025), Development and validation of a learning analytics rubric for SRL. https://link.springer.com/article/10.1007/s11423-025-10521-x. **Relevance:** Validated construct/rubric study baseline.
- `HISTORY2025` — Regrounding Inquiry-Based Learning in History (2025). https://www.tandfonline.com/doi/full/10.1080/07370008.2025.2503193. **Relevance:** Historical inquiry processes and sourcing/appraising prior art.
- `WCAG2024` — W3C WCAG 2.2 Recommendation. https://www.w3.org/TR/wcag/. **Relevance:** Normative requirements, not novelty.
- `CAST2024` — CAST Universal Design for Learning Guidelines 3.0. https://udlguidelines.cast.org/more/about-guidelines-3-0/. **Relevance:** UDL is not equivalent to automatable WCAG.
- `GENA11Y2025` — Enhancing Web Accessibility: Automated Detection of Issues with Generative AI (FSE 2025). https://seal.ics.uci.edu/publications/2025_FSE_GenA11y.pdf. **Relevance:** Prior work on LLM accessibility audit coverage.
- `A11Y2026` — An empirical investigation of Large Language Models for automated software compliance testing (2026). https://doi.org/10.1016/j.infsof.2026.108215. **Relevance:** Competitive tool+LLM WCAG testing comparisons.
- `AWS_IDEMPOTENCY` — AWS Well Architected, Make mutating operations idempotent. https://docs.aws.amazon.com/wellarchitected/latest/framework/rel_prevent_interaction_failure_idempotent.html. **Relevance:** Exactly-once semantics is an engineering foundation, not novelty alone.
- `WILDS` — WILDS: A Benchmark of in-the-Wild Distribution Shifts (ICML 2021). https://proceedings.mlr.press/v139/koh21a.html. **Relevance:** OOD benchmark evaluation principles.
- `CMDKT2025` — Enhancing knowledge tracing through course map and question difficulty analysis (2025). https://www.sciencedirect.com/science/article/pii/S259000562500150X. **Relevance:** Skill graph+question difficulty already studied.
- `KT_LEAK2026` — Enhancing Knowledge Tracing through Leakage-Free and Recency-Aware Embeddings (2026). https://link.springer.com/article/10.1007/s42979-026-05344-3. **Relevance:** Recent leakage-aware KT benchmark rival.
