# P06 Manuscript Skeleton

**Title (working).** Budget-Constrained Teacher Intervention Prioritization:
Ranking At-Risk Learners Under Equity Constraints — Without Causal Claims

**Venue (target, unverified).** Learning@Scale (CORE B). Verify CFP, 2027 rank
and fit before submission. **Status: NONCAUSAL_UNTIL_PROVED.**

---

## 1. Research Question

With a fixed teacher capacity of k interventions per selection round, can
uncertainty-aware, equity-constrained prioritization improve *identification
and coverage* of at-risk learners over random selection (C0) and calibrated
risk-only top-k (C1) — **without claiming any intervention effect**?

- **Estimand.** Selection quality against realized outcomes: failure capture
  (Recall@k) of the selected set within each course-presentation cohort.
- **H0 (preregistered).** Constrained ranking adds no material benefit over
  calibrated top-K risk ranking.
- **Result (headline, honest).** **H0 NOT REJECTED.** See §6.

## 4. Method (see research_v3/methods/p06/METHOD.md for the frozen contract)

Calibrated logistic risk (class_weight balanced; sigmoid calibration on the
calibration partition via FrozenEstimator) + budgeted selection:

- **Eligibility gates.** ABSTAIN for zero-evidence learners (never selected,
  even at maximal risk — LN-09 mirror); NOT_APPLICABLE for risk < 0.05.
- **Objective.** max Σ p_i s.t. |S| ≤ k, equity floors
  m_g = ceil(0.5 · π_g · k) per disability group (π_g = eligible-pool share).
  Two-phase greedy; exact for this separable objective; O(n log n).
- **Reporting privacy.** k-anonymity: per-subgroup cells with n < 5 suppressed.

## 5. Experimental setup

Arms: C0 random-eligible, C1 risk-only top-k, PROPOSED (floors+gates),
A1 raw margins (no calibration), A2 no capacity, A3 no gates, A4 no floors.
k ∈ {25, 50, 100}. One selection round per test cohort (7 cohorts, time split;
22 cohorts, group split). Paired exact McNemar per cohort×k, cluster bootstrap
(learner clusters, 2,000 resamples), Holm across the preregistered 6-test
family. Wilson + Clopper–Pearson CIs. All artifacts regenerated byte-for-byte
(verified; `rerun_check.json`).

## 6. Results (tables from frozen artifacts — tables/main_results.md)

**Headline finding (null result, published per preregistration).** PROPOSED vs
C1: **no material difference in failure capture at any k** (identical pooled
Recall@k to 3 decimals at k=50 and k=100; −0.002 at k=25, CIs overlapping;
all Holm-corrected McNemar p = 1.0). **Mechanism (measured, honest):** on real
OULAD the risk-only ranker already over-selects the disability subgroup
relative to its eligible-pool share (e.g. BBB/2014J, k=25: 6/25 = 24% selected
vs 9.4% pool share), so the proportional equity floor is never binding —
the floors formalize what calibrated risk already does on this data. The
equity machinery is therefore *not harmful* (zero capture cost at k=50/100)
but adds **no material benefit** on OULAD. External generalization: single
dataset — **unverified**.

**Ablations.** A1 (no calibration) degrades capture at k=25
(0.110→0.110 pooled but per-round NDCG 0.461→0.460, ECE 0.050→0.412);
A3 (no gates) *lowers* capture (0.099 vs 0.110 at k=25) while admitting
418 zero-evidence learners per large cohort — quantifying the LN-09 gate's
protective value; A2 (no capacity) inflates recall trivially (0.935) by
selecting everyone eligible with precision collapsing to prevalence (0.201);
A4 (no floors) ≡ C1 (floors never bind).

**Fairness gap.** Pooled coverage gap (Y−N) ranges −0.012…+0.805 across k,
identical for PROPOSED and C1 — the gap reflects risk structure, not the
policy. Small selected cells are suppressed (n<5), so several cohort-level
gaps are reported as None rather than fabricated numbers.

## 7. Threats to Validity (required by spec §7; causality boundary verbatim
in research_v3/methods/p06/noncausal_claims.md)

1. **Observational data — no causal claim.** No intervention was assigned or
   delivered. All metrics measure agreement between a selection policy and
   *already realized* outcomes. We make no claim that intervening on a
   selected learner would improve their outcome. Estimating intervention
   effectiveness requires an RCT or propensity-based identification — out of
   scope. (Boundary stated in Methods and Limitations; enforced by claims
   ledger + `noncausal_attestation.json`.)
2. **Single external dataset.** OULAD only; external generalization unverified.
3. **Label semantics.** `Withdrawn` excluded by preregistration; failure among
   withdrawers is unobserved. Sensitivity to this exclusion not yet run.
4. **Few test cohorts.** 7 cohorts (time split); cluster bootstrap CIs wide;
   McNemar p-values at cohort granularity are conservative (Holm p = 1.0).
5. **Floor design.** Proportional floors (0.5·π_g·k) never bind when calibrated
   risk already over-selects the disadvantaged group; on a dataset where risk
   under-selects it, behavior would differ — untested here.
6. **Feature horizon.** Day-60 cut-off chosen a priori; other horizons untested.
7. **Classical model only.** XGBoost/LightGBM baselines environment-BLOCKED
   (not installed in the pinned environment); the C0/C1/PROPOSED contrast does
   not depend on them, but predictor-strength sensitivity is unmeasured.
